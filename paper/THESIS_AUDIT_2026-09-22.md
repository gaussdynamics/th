## 2. The formulation change, and everything that depends on it

Three things were conflated under one phrase. Only the first survives.

| Element | Status |
|---|---|
| GNN / message passing over the consist chain | **Kept, unchanged** |
| Physics-residual `ŷ = y_phys + Δy_GNN` | **Dropped** — the residual is *algebraically zero*, because `data/v2` was generated by integrating `simulator2.torch_rhs`, which **is** `y_phys` |
| Neural ODE | **Dropped**, demoted to related work — coupler stiffness forces `h ≤ 0.05 s`, so ~5 network calls per stored transition against states no data can supervise |

The replacement is a **graph network simulator**: one learned fixed 0.25 s step.
The network predicts mean acceleration only; position comes from the kinematic
trapezoid plus a small learned shape correction, and the actuator lags from the
exact analytic solution of `ż = (u − z)/τ`. One network call replaces 50 RHS
evaluations.

Worth carrying into the prose: **imposed physics goes up, not down**. The
network's output narrows from four channels per vehicle to two scalars.

## 5. Framing decision — how the abandoned design is handled

**Settled 2026-09-22: exclude the narrative, include the argument, acknowledge
the change once.**

### Why not simply omit it

`trb_paper/ltd_surrogate_trb.tex:185` commits to the residual in print, under the
author's name:

> "The surrogate does not learn $\mathcal{F}_n$ from a blank slate. It begins from
> the known physics right-hand side and learns a correction, so that the predicted
> rate of change takes the form $\mathcal{F}_n + \ldots$"

The old design is therefore already external. A thesis that silently contradicts
a submitted paper reads worse than one that states the change, so writing as
though the residual was never the plan is not available.

### Why not narrate it either

A thesis is an argument, not a lab notebook. Telling the story invites the reader
to evaluate the *process* rather than the *design*, and it subtly signals that the
final architecture was arrived at by elimination rather than by principle. The
measurements are strong enough to stand as forward justification on their own.

### Where each piece goes

| Chapter | What it carries |
|---|---|
| **3 — Formulation** | Only the current formulation, derived forward. No history, no hedging. A formulation chapter that tells a story is a weak formulation chapter. |
| **2 — Related Work** | Neural ODEs and physics-residual surrogates as literature, described neutrally. This is where they are established as the natural alternatives. |
| **7 — Architecture** | The measured rejection, framed as **design justification, not confession**. The question posed is *"why not anchor the step on the physics right-hand side?"*, answered with the anchor table, `ω·h = 7.45` against RK4's ~2.8 limit, and couplers outside the deadband 91.1% of the time. The zero-residual argument belongs here too. |
| **9 — Evaluation** | Neural ODE and residual MLP as baseline rows. "We tried it, here is why it loses on this problem." |
| **10 — Discussion** | One paragraph on the transferable lesson: a physics residual is the right design when `y_phys` *approximates* data from elsewhere; it is degenerate when `y_phys` *is* the generating process. |
| **1 — Introduction** *(or a Ch. 3 footnote)* | **One sentence**, forward-pointing, no apology: *"An earlier statement of this work [TRB cite] proposed a physics-residual formulation; §7.2 gives the measurement that led to its replacement."* |

### The test to apply sentence by sentence

**Would this sentence still belong if the residual had never been tried?**

The anchor table would — it is how the decision *not* to anchor is justified,
regardless of what was tried first. "We originally planned…" would not. Keep the
first kind; compress the second into the single acknowledgment sentence.

### Two things to get right while writing it

- **Lead with "imposed physics goes up, not down."** Two of four state channels
  leave the network entirely and the output narrows to two scalars per vehicle.
  Without that framing the pivot reads as a retreat, which it is not.
- **Scope the zero-residual claim.** It holds because the surrogate and the data
  generator are the same model. Stated more broadly than that it is wrong, and an
  examiner who works on residual methods will say so.

### Naming

Call it a **graph network simulator**, after Sanchez-Gonzalez et al. Do not call
it a Neural ODE or a physics-residual anywhere in the document; it is neither, and
the mismatch is exactly what an examiner will find.

(`surrogate/`, 16 tests, trains in ~80 s on the 4090). Material available:

  argument; the stiffness measurement (`ω·h = 7.45` against RK4's ~2.8 limit;

  (velocity 5.8× *worse* with any physics anchor, position 113× *better* with the
  trapezoid); message-passing depth set by wave speed rather than tuning; and the
  float32 quantization finding that moved the correction head from nodes to edges
  (signal-to-quantization 0.77 in `state.x` against 117,713 in `edge_dynamic`).
- **Chapter 8 (Training).** Noise injection and pushforward, and the measured
  result that they are complementary rather than substitutes.
- **Chapter 9 (Evaluation).** Velocity extrapolates to consists 1.5–15× longer
  than trained on; **coupler shape does not** (1.2× over hold on 150-car consists
  against 6.8× on val), diagnosed as a stability rather than accuracy problem,
  with three hypotheses tested and rejected.
- **Chapter 10 (Control).** Still genuinely blocked. `control_eval` is empty, and
  the safety constraint lives on coupler force, on long consists, over long
  horizons — precisely the corner where shape does not extrapolate.
