## ⚠ The formulation changed — the deck is built against the superseded design

**Branch `surrogate-gns` (11 commits ahead of `main`, unmerged) replaced the
physics-residual Neural-ODE design on 2026-09-17.** `main` — which this deck and
the thesis chapters are written from — still describes the old one. Full record
in `SURROGATE_FORMULATION_NOTE.md` on that branch; commit `ff42571`.

Three things were conflated under one phrase. Only one survives:

| Element | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GNN / message passing | **Kept** | Nodes = vehicles, edges = couplers. Carries the variable-`N` claim structurally. Never in question. |
| Physics-residual `ŷ = y_phys + Δy_GNN` | **Dropped** | The residual is *algebraically zero*: `data/v2` was generated by integrating `simulator2.torch_rhs`, which **is** `y_phys`. A residual needs data from somewhere the physics model is not; here it is the exact generating process. Separately, it would be strictly slower than the simulator it replaces. |
| Neural ODE | **Dropped**, demoted to related work | Coupler stiffness forces `h ≤ 0.05` s, so ~5 network calls per stored transition, producing intermediate states no stored data can supervise. |

**The replacement:** a graph network simulator taking one learned fixed 0.25 s
step. The network predicts mean acceleration only; position comes from the
kinematic trapezoid and the actuator lags from the exact analytic solution of
`ż = (u − z)/τ`. One network call replaces **50 RHS evaluations** (`dt = 0.02`
internal, `dt_s = 0.25` stored), which is where the speedup actually lives.

Note the framing the note insists on: imposed physics went **up**, not down —
the network's output narrows from four channels per vehicle to two scalars.
Name it a graph network simulator, not a Neural ODE and not a physics-residual.

### One thing to check, not assume

Ch. 3 justifies the smooth brake `tanh` blend and the smooth `linear` curvature
law partly as keeping "an RHS the surrogate differentiates through"
well-conditioned. The surrogate no longer differentiates through the RHS at all.
Those choices are still defensible — smooth data is easier to learn, and the MPC
differentiates through the *surrogate* — but the stated reason needs rewording
rather than repeating.

### New material the deck plan does not yet have

From the `surrogate-gns` work: measured stiffness (`ω·h = 7.45` against RK4's
~2.8 limit; couplers outside the slack deadband 91% of the time); the trapezoid
being exact on bulk motion (0.7 mm) but off 4.3 cm on the relative displacement
the coupler law reads; the negative result that the 0.25 s grid does *not* alias
peak coupler force (0.4% median underestimate); velocity extrapolating to
consists 1.5–15× longer than trained on; rollout surviving 200 steps with noise
injection plus pushforward; and **one unresolved limitation** — coupler *shape*
does not extrapolate on long rollouts (1.2× over hold on 150-car consists
against 7× on val), with three hypotheses tested and rejected.
